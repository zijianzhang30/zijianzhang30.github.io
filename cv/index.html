Research

GaussianDream++

2026.04 – 2026.07

Co-first second author

An action-compatible 3D world model for vision-language-action control.

  • Designed world state tokens, world prediction tokens, and a coupled Gaussian world head for compact 3D world modeling inside VLA policies.
  • Used static-dynamic residual transition modeling with RGB, depth, alpha, and 3D motion supervision during training, while removing Gaussian decoding modules at inference.
  • Achieved 98.6% average success rate on LIBERO and improved spatial generalization in LIBERO-Plus and real-robot settings.

GaussianWAM

2026.04 – 2026.07

Co-first first author

Distilling geometry and semantics from 3D Gaussian fields into world-action models.

  • Proposed a Gaussian Field Enhancement framework to inject 3D geometry and visual semantics into action-relevant WAM representations.
  • Bound geometry and semantic features to 3D Gaussian primitives, then distilled rendered semantic, depth, and alpha signals into policy representations.
  • Improved FastWAM on LIBERO-Plus from 52.05% to 70.99% and Cosmos Policy from 71.52% to 77.30% with no extra inference overhead.

GaussianDream

2025.11 – 2026.05

Co-first author

A feed-forward 3D Gaussian world model for robotic manipulation.

  • Built a 3D Gaussian world model plug-in for VLA policies to learn current scene structure and short-horizon future dynamics from robot trajectories.
  • Introduced GaussianDream queries and a temporal 3D-aware encoder with current Gaussian reconstruction and future Gaussian prediction objectives.
  • Achieved 98.4% average success on LIBERO, 54.8% on RoboCasa Human-50, and 50.0% on real-robot tasks.

Fuse and Refine

2025.10 – 2026.03

First author and independent lead

Robust and accurate HD map auto-labeling for large-scale autonomous-driving scenes.

  • Developed an auto-labeling pipeline for static road elements from multimodal perception to vectorized HD map annotations.
  • Built training and evaluation pipelines on self-collected data, using VMA as the baseline and adding Sp-Net for robust coarse detection.
  • Projected initial predictions back to raw point clouds for local sampling and used Refine-Net to correct geometric position and shape errors.
