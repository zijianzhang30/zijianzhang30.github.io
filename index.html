About Me

I am a master’s student in Computer Application Technology at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree in Robotics Engineering from the University of Science and Technology Beijing.

My research focuses on robot learning, world models, vision-language-action models, and 3D perception. I am interested in how robots represent the physical world, anticipate its dynamics, and turn perception into reliable action.

I have worked on 3D Gaussian world models, contact-rich manipulation benchmarks, HD map auto-labeling, and active LiDAR perception.
