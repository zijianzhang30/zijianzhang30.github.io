I am **Zijian Zhang (张梓健)**, an incoming M.S. student in Computer Application Technology at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, affiliated with the School of Artificial Intelligence. Before that, I received my B.Eng. training in Robotics Engineering at the University of Science and Technology Beijing.

My research interests lie in **robot learning**, **vision-language-action models**, **world-action models**, **3D world modeling**, and **3D perception**. I am especially interested in how robots can build compact geometric and semantic representations of the world, predict short-horizon physical changes, and use these representations for efficient closed-loop manipulation.

I have also worked on autonomous-driving perception and robotic systems, including BEVFusion deployment on mining-vehicle platforms, software-defined LiDAR for active 3D perception, HD map auto-labeling, and ROBOCON robot control systems.

**Research directions:** robot learning, VLA models, world-action models, 3D Gaussian world models, multimodal perception, active 3D perception.
